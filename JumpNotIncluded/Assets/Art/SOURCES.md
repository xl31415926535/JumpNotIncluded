## World Three castle

Basalt bricks, furnace plates, cracks, lava, fire chains, crushers, jets, windows and banners are original procedural pixel sprites generated by `Assets/Scripts/CastleArt.cs`. The third level uses an original layout inspired by Nintendo's castle/lava/obstacle combinations in Super Mario Maker; no downloaded player level is included. Its route and mechanics are documented in `docs/world3-design.md` in the repository root.

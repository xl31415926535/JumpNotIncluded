# Advertisement artwork

These images are bundled locally so the simulated ads work offline. Their source files are copied without raster edits.

## SL Cheater

- `slcheater-war-god.png`: the user's SL Cheater project, `assets/art/portraits-v07/war-god.png` (War God / orbital assault chassis promotional illustration).
- Reused at the user's request. This is the full promotional illustration, not a replacement for SL Cheater's current pixel-game portrait.

Unity imports the promotional artwork with bilinear sampling and preserves its aspect ratio. The game does not contact an ad service or make real payments.

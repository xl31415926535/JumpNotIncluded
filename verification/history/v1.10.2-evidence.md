# v1.10.2 mushroom and Star audio fixes

The growth pickup previously referenced the green extra-life mushroom at atlas
x=52. ProjectSetup now selects the red growth mushroom at x=71; serialized sprite
data is updated. Asset migration advances revision 8 to 9 while retaining GUIDs.

The Star music file already existed but was not selected during invincibility.
AudioDirector now switches the looping Music source to 05-starman, records the
level playhead and resumes it on expiry. Repeated pickups refresh the buff without
restarting playback. Pause, death, overworld/underground tracks and mute are covered.

The final source files are copied directly from the current v1.10.2 project. Nine
additional runtime checks produce the retained 145-check report. The hill-large
sprite render-data key also changed during Unity's asset import; this serialized
metadata change is included without describing it as a separate gameplay feature.
